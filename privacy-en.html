2. Scope — the short version

  • The app requires no account and no sign-in.
  • The app does not collect your personal data and does not profile users. It does not use location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, health data or advertising.
  • Once downloaded, the games run offline; content and settings (e.g. player names, spice level, choice of forfeits) are saved locally on your device and are never sent to us.
  • Payments and subscriptions are handled by Apple within the App Store. It is Apple, not us, that processes transaction and payment data (see section 4).
  • We process personal data only when you contact us yourself (e.g. a support e-mail or a bug report) — in that case we process the data you send us (see section 5).
This policy is consistent with the “App Privacy” declaration in the App Store: the app is configured as “Data Not Collected” (the app does not collect data). Any purchase-related data is collected by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Simply using the app (playing) means that we process no personal data at all on our servers. The table below covers the only situations in which data is processed — and shows who the controller is.

4. Payments and subscriptions (Apple)

All in-app purchases (a single game, Lifetime access, the weekly subscription) are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. This means that:

  • billing runs through your Apple ID account;
  • we neither see nor store your card details or full billing data — they are processed by Apple as the payment service provider;
  • how Apple processes data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy;
  • you manage your subscription (including cancellation) and any refunds in your Apple ID / App Store settings — details in the Terms (§ 6).

From Apple we receive only aggregate, statistical sales information (e.g. the number of purchases), which does not allow any individual user to be identified.

5. Support contact and bug reports

When you e-mail us (support, a question, a complaint, a bug report), we process your e-mail address and the content of your message — solely in order to reply and handle the matter. Providing this data is voluntary, but necessary for us to be able to get back to you.

We handle correspondence using e-mail provided by Google (Gmail). We keep your e-mail for as long as it is needed to handle the matter and for the limitation period for any potential claims.

6. Third-party services and SDKs

To the best of our knowledge as at the date of publication, the app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase, PostHog, ad networks, pixels). In particular:

  • we do not use tracking for advertising purposes (App Tracking Transparency / IDFA);
  • we do not use advertising SDKs;
  • we do not use AI/ML solutions that process your data;
  • we do not embed cookies or similar technologies for marketing purposes.

The only third parties involved in the operation of the service are Apple (app distribution and payments) and Google (Gmail) as our e-mail provider (handling contact).

7. Sharing of data

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may be processed only by:

  • Apple — for app distribution and payment handling;
  • Google (Gmail) — as our e-mail provider, if you contact us;
  • competent authorities — only where required by law.

Using Apple’s and Google’s services may involve transfers of data outside the European Economic Area (including to the USA). Both providers state that they apply the transfer mechanisms provided for in the GDPR, in particular the standard contractual clauses (SCC) approved by the European Commission, and both Apple and Google participate in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details in the privacy policies of Apple and Google.

8. Retention periods

  • In-game data (player names, settings) — stored locally on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • E-mail correspondence — for as long as the matter is being handled and for the limitation period for claims.
  • Transaction data relating to purchases — held by Apple, in line with Apple’s policies and accounting regulations.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

To the extent that we process your data (i.e. when you contact us), you have the right to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, object to processing and — where processing is based on consent — withdraw that consent.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (PUODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland.

To exercise your rights, write to oyey.games@gmail.com.

10. Withdrawing consent

As a rule, the app does not operate on the basis of consent (it collects no data). If features requiring consent appear in the future (e.g. a marketing newsletter), you will be able to withdraw your consent at any time — without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal — by writing to oyey.games@gmail.com or using the relevant option in the message or in the app.

11. Deleting data and accounts

The app does not allow you to create an account, so there is no account to delete. To remove data stored locally, simply clear the app’s data or uninstall it. To have the e-mail correspondence you sent us deleted, write to oyey.games@gmail.com. Details: Data deletion.

12. Age of users

The app is intended solely for adults (18+) — that is also its age rating in the App Store. Inside the app you can additionally switch off the most explicit cards with the “18+ cards” toggle in settings. The app is not directed at minors and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy (e.g. when the app’s features or our providers change). The current version is published at https://oyey.eu/privacy-en.html together with its effective date. We will flag any material changes in the app or on the website.
