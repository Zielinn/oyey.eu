Creator programme

Your code, a free game for your audience, a share of their purchases

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 6 August 2026

In short: you get your own code. Whoever redeems it claims a full game for free, and you get 50% of our proceeds from everything that person buys in Oyey over the next 12 months. No exclusivity, no follower thresholds.

This page is the programme's terms. You join by writing to us and claiming your code — from that moment they bind both of us.

1. The rules

  • Reward for your audience: the full "Girls' Night" game, forever, no subscription. The code works for everyone, including people who have already bought something in Oyey.
  • Your share: 50% of our proceeds from every purchase that person makes within 12 months of redeeming the code.
  • Refunds are deducted. If Apple refunds someone, that purchase drops out of the settlement.
  • Monthly settlement, once Apple closes the month. We pay against an invoice, or against a bill (Polish rachunek) if you are not in business (see point 5).
  • Apple is not a party to this arrangement and does not handle the payouts.

2. What that actually comes to

"Proceeds" means what we really receive from Apple: the store price after VAT and after the App Store commission. We spell the numbers out, because a percentage of the gross price would overstate them by roughly a quarter.

That is the same rule Polish law applies to revenue in a foreign currency, so the settlement can be checked in both directions. Your report shows both: the amount in the sale currency and the rate, together with the NBP table number used to convert it.

4. How we count referrals

The twelve months run from the day someone redeems your code. We record that referral on our side permanently: it does not lapse along the way and it does not depend on whether your code is still active.

We settle purchases made on the same Apple account that redeemed your code. That comes with one limitation and we say it plainly: if someone redeems the code on one Apple account but buys on another (a family member's, say), we have no way to connect the two and that purchase will not enter the settlement.

Everything we count comes from the notifications Apple sends us after each transaction — we have no other source of data and we never fill in the gaps by guesswork. If you believe a purchase by your audience was not counted, write to us: we will check the referral record and show you what we see in it.

5. Payouts: when and against what

  • The report for the previous month goes out by the 10th of the following month. Any earlier and the data is incomplete, because Apple closes a month after it ends.
  • What we pay against: an invoice if you are in business, or a bill (Polish rachunek) if you are not. No document, no payout — that is an accounting requirement, not a whim of ours.
  • Term: bank transfer within 14 days of receiving a correct document.
  • Minimum payout: PLN 100. If a month comes to less, the balance rolls over to the next month and waits until the threshold is passed. When the cooperation ends we pay out the whole balance regardless of the threshold.
  • The form of cooperation and the tax obligations (invoice or a bill under a contract, who pays advances and contributions) are agreed with you before the first payout. If you are settling from outside Poland, we agree it individually.

6. What you get in the report

The number of people who redeemed your code and the total amount owed to you. We pass you no data about individual people or transactions and never will: that data is not ours to hand out. Your audience's privacy is described in the Privacy Policy, section 5.

7. What we never do

  • We never pay for downloads, installs, ratings or reviews. The share is counted from actual purchases only.
  • We never make a reward conditional on leaving a rating, watching an ad or enabling notifications.
  • Codes must not be sold or shared for a fee, including in paid communities (see Terms, § 5).

8. Disclosing the partnership

This is a commercial partnership, so it must be disclosed in your content as required by the law of your country. In practice: say plainly that you get a share.

9. Who can join

You join in your own name, not on behalf of a company or another person, and you must be at least 18 years old. Oyey is a game for adults, and the reward in the programme is a deck labelled as adult content ("Girls' Night"), so the material in which you promote the code must be aimed at an adult audience. Do not put the code in content marked as made for kids, and if the platform lets you mark a video as intended for adults, use it.

We require no exclusivity, no minimum reach and no set number of posts.

10. Our brand in your content

For the duration of the cooperation you may use the Oyey logo, our mascot and screenshots from the app in the material where you recommend the game. The licence is non-exclusive, non-transferable and granted for that purpose only. We ask two things: do not alter the logo or the mascot (no redrawing, no new expressions, no changing colours or proportions), and do not imply that you publish Oyey or that we sponsor anything other than our own game. The brand stays ours and the licence ends with the cooperation — content you have already published can stay up, we do not ask anyone to delete videos.

The other way round: we do not use your name, handle or content in our advertising without your consent. The exception is the code itself and the fact that you are in the programme.

11. Ending the cooperation

Either side may end the cooperation at any time, by a plain e-mail, without giving a reason and without a notice period.

  • The code stops working for new people — we deactivate it in the App Store.
  • Nobody loses the game they claimed. Codes redeemed earlier stay with those people forever.
  • The accrual continues. Referrals recorded before the deactivation are settled to the end of their twelve-month window and paid out as usual, on the terms in point 5.
  • An unused code may be deactivated by us to free one of the ten slots (see point 14). We give you notice by e-mail, and the accrual from earlier referrals carries on.

We may end the cooperation immediately and withhold a payout if the code is sold or shared for a fee, promoted misleadingly or in content aimed at minors, or if redemptions are generated artificially. We will always write to you saying exactly what we are questioning and give you a chance to reply before anything is forfeited.

12. Your data

To pay you we need your details: your name or company name, address, tax number (NIP) or personal number (PESEL) and bank account number. We process them to perform this agreement and to meet our accounting and tax obligations, and we keep accounting documents for 5 years from the end of the tax year. Full details: Privacy Policy, section 6.

We may change these terms. A new version is published on this page with its number and effective date, and we give you at least 14 days' notice by e-mail. Referrals recorded before a change are settled on the terms in force when the code was redeemed.
