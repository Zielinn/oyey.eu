Creator programme

Your code, a free game for your audience, a share of their purchases

In short: you get your own code. Whoever redeems it claims a full game for free, and you get 50% of our proceeds from everything that person buys in Oyey over the next 12 months. No exclusivity, no follower thresholds.

1. The rules

  • Reward for your audience: the full "Girls' Night" game, forever, no subscription. The code works for everyone, including people who have already bought something in Oyey.
  • Your share: 50% of our proceeds from every purchase that person makes within 12 months of redeeming the code.
  • Refunds are deducted. If Apple refunds someone, that purchase drops out of the settlement.
  • Monthly settlement, once Apple closes the month. We pay against an invoice.
  • Apple is not a party to this arrangement and does not handle the payouts.

2. What that actually comes to

"Proceeds" means what we really receive from Apple: the store price after VAT and after the App Store commission. We spell the numbers out, because a percentage of the gross price would overstate them by roughly a quarter.

In other countries the ratio is similar but not identical, because VAT differs. We always compute it from Apple's price list for that storefront, never from a fixed multiplier.

3. What you get in the report

The number of people who redeemed your code and the total amount owed to you. We pass you no data about individual people or transactions and never will: that data is not ours to hand out. Your audience's privacy is described in the Privacy Policy, section 5.

4. What we never do

  • We never pay for downloads, installs, ratings or reviews. The share is counted from actual purchases only.
  • We never make a reward conditional on leaving a rating, watching an ad or enabling notifications.
  • Codes must not be sold or shared for a fee, including in paid communities (see Terms, § 5).

5. Disclosing the partnership

This is a commercial partnership, so it must be disclosed in your content as required by the law of your country. In practice: say plainly that you get a share.

6. How to join

Write to oyey.games@gmail.com. One honest warning: Apple lets us keep 10 active offer codes across the whole app, so places are genuinely scarce. If a code stops being used we free the slot, and referrals already made with that code keep being settled.
